Activities

What We Offer

Filter by type or browse everything. Meeting points are confirmed on our socials before each session.

Social Runs

Easy-paced group runs focused on conversation and community. Routes vary weekly. Perfect for meeting other members and exploring the area at a comfortable effort.

All Levels ~45–60 min Main Park Entrance

Speed Training

Structured intervals, tempo efforts, and track sessions. Designed to improve VO2 max, lactate threshold, and race-day sharpness. Coaches provide effort guidance.

Moderate+ ~60–75 min Athletics Track

Long-Distance Runs

Weekend long runs building aerobic endurance. Distances scale with the group’s goals — ideal for half and full marathon preparation. Support vehicles on select routes.

Experienced 90–180 min Trailhead Car Park

Beginner-Friendly Sessions

Walk-run intervals, technique tips, and a welcoming atmosphere. No experience required. We start slow and build confidence together. Ideal for new runners or returning after a break.

Beginner ~40–50 min Main Park Entrance

Strength & Mobility

Optional conditioning sessions focusing on injury prevention, core strength, and mobility. Bodyweight and light equipment. Complements your running load.

All Levels ~45 min Park or Indoor Hall

Recovery Runs

Short, very easy-paced runs prioritising active recovery. Great between hard sessions or as a gentle mid-week option. Conversation pace only.

Easy ~30–40 min Main Park Entrance

Session times and meeting points can change with the weather and daylight. Check our social pages for the latest before you head out.
